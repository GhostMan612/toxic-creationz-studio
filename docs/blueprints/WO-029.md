### B. TR-808 — Faithful Layout & Active Voice Levels (`src/HardwareRackPlugins.h`, `src/HardwarePluginEditors.h/.cpp`)
1. **`Tr808DrumPlugin` (`src/HardwareRackPlugins.h`):** Keep the existing 5 parameters (`kickDecay`, `kickTone`, `snareSnappy`, `hatDecay`, `accent`) and add 5 real per-voice level `AutomatableParameter` + `CachedValue<float>` pairs (`kickLevel`, `snareLevel`, `lowTomLevel`, `midTomLevel`, `hatLevel`, range `0.0..1.5`, default `1.0`) applied to the 6 synthesized voices in `applyToBuffer()`. Detach all 10 parameters in `~Tr808DrumPlugin()` and sync all 10 via `updateFromAttachedValue()` in `restorePluginStateFromValueTree()`.
2. **`Tr808Editor` (`src/HardwarePluginEditors.h/.cpp`):** Re-lay to the real machine's 11-column voice strip in authentic color groups and order (`AC`, `BD`, `SD`, `LT/LC`, `MT/MC`, `HT/HC`, `RS/CL`, `CP/MA`, `CB`, `CY`, `OH/CH`):
   - Active voice columns (`AC`, `BD`, `SD`, `LT`, `MT`, `OH/CH`) carry live `juce::Slider` knobs bound 1-to-1 to all 10 real `AutomatableParameter`s.
   - Unimplemented voice columns (`HT/HC`, `RS/CL`, `CP/MA`, `CB`, `CY`) render as authentic recessed/disabled silkscreened dummy caps (painted vector caps, **not** `juce::Slider`s) so the 11-column silhouette is faithful while every `juce::Slider` binds to a real parameter.
   - Render the **16-step button row** across the bottom in classic 4×4 red / orange / yellow / white color groups. The step row is **display-only in this WO**: expose `void syncFromPlayheadBeats (double beats, bool isPlaying)` (called from `timerCallback()` during live playback) to light the current 16th-note step LED (`0..15`); buttons do not edit, store, or trigger state.
   - Keep the 6 live audition trigger pads (`KICK`, `SNARE`, `CH`, `OH`, `LT`, `MT`), positioned cleanly along the hardware layout.
